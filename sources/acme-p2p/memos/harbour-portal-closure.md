# Harbour closure note
Client: Acme
Subject: procure-to-pay
Date: 2026-03-14
From: Dana Okonkwo, procurement lead
To: Ellis Ward, controller

## Decision

The supplier-portal project, called Harbour, was cancelled and no supplier can submit an invoice through a portal.

## What the pilot was

Harbour was a supplier portal pilot. It opened on 2025-06-02 with two suppliers, Bramble
Tin and Lowlamp Paper. A supplier could type an invoice into Harbour. A clerk then
re-keyed the same invoice into Paywindow and compared the three documents on the match
log. The pilot did not remove that re-keying.

## Why it stopped

The year's money for the pilot stopped. I will not ask the controller for more. The
re-keying is the reason, not a supplier complaint.

## What suppliers were told

Both pilot suppliers, and any supplier who asks, are told to keep sending invoices to
invoices@acme.example.

## Access

On 2026-03-21 the remaining Harbour logins were removed. Nothing in production still
accepts a supplier document through that pilot. The system inventory does not list
retired pilots. This note is the record of the decision.
